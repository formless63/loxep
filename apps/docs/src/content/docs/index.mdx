Loxep is an open-source, self-hosted platform being designed around three connected ideas: **market intelligence**, **business operations**, and **financial truth**.

The initial implementation centers on eBay monitoring: watches, searches, sellers, prices, availability, restocks, and historical observations. The architecture is deliberately broader so those capabilities can grow into multichannel commerce, inventory, service work, billing, shipping, purchasing, accounting, tax support, and reporting without becoming a collection of disconnected tools.

Loxep is documentation-first while its foundations are being established. The master domain map captures the territory; the roadmap determines what actually gets built and when.
