---
title: Vision
---

Loxep is an open-source, self-hosted platform for understanding and operating the commercial activity of individuals and small businesses.

It begins with a concrete problem: continuously observe marketplaces such as eBay, detect meaningful changes quickly, retain the history that marketplaces do not expose conveniently, and turn those observations into useful actions. That first use case naturally connects to the other side of commerce: products acquired are inventoried, listed, sold, shipped, paid out, and ultimately reflected in the books.

The same operational foundation also applies outside marketplace selling. A small operator may sell physical goods through eBay, WooCommerce, or Medusa while also performing consulting, development, hosting, installation, integration, repair, or other service work. Loxep should be able to represent those activities without pretending they are all the same workflow.

## Product direction

Loxep aims to connect several domains that are usually fragmented across unrelated applications:

- marketplace monitoring and historical intelligence;
- multichannel commerce and listings;
- purchasing, inventory, fulfillment, and shipping;
- customers, projects, time, and service delivery;
- recurring services and subscriptions;
- quotes, invoices, payments, and receivables;
- expenses, payouts, fees, and banking reconciliation;
- double-entry accounting and financial reporting;
- sales-tax facts and income-tax-oriented reporting;
- documents and operational records.

The goal is not to build every possible ERP feature. The goal is to maintain a coherent model of **what actually happened in the business**, preserve the underlying evidence, and allow specialized workflows to build on that truth.

## Why self-hosted

Loxep should be straightforward for an individual, reseller, consultant, installer, developer, or small business to deploy without becoming dependent on a hosted Loxep service. Integrations will necessarily depend on external providers, but the user's operational history and derived intelligence should remain theirs.

## Open source

Loxep is intended to be released under the MIT license. Generic deployment, multiple connections/accounts, documented APIs, migrations, backups, and upgrade paths are therefore product requirements rather than afterthoughts.

## Name

**Loxep** combines *loxodrome* and *ephemeris*.

A loxodrome is a navigational path of constant bearing. An ephemeris records positions or values over time. Together they reflect the two central ideas behind the project: navigating commercial activity and observing how its state changes over time.
